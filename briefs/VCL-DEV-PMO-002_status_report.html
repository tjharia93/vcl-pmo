How it works — the buttons in order

The whole system is driven by six buttons in the PMO page at /app/pmo. Below are the actual buttons in the order you click them, from creating a plan to handing it to an agent. Each button is shown the same colour it appears in the live system: navy for primary action, amber for the brand action (Slack), white outline for secondary.

Figure 1. The six clicks from idea to running shift. Navy buttons are primary actions; amber is the brand action; outline buttons are secondary.

Where each piece lives

We deliberately do not try to put everything in one tool. The principle is two anchors: ERPNext is the operational source of truth for project work, and the Slack channel + Excel mirror exist for reading and review surfaces that already work for the team.

Figure 2. One source of truth, two downstream review surfaces. No reverse writes from Slack or Excel back into ERPNext.
